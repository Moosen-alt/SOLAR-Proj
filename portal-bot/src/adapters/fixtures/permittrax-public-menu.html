UPLOAD PORTAL

Welcome to the MyPermits plans and documents upload portal.

First-time users of this system will need to establish an account through the "Sign In" hyperlink located at the top right of the MyPermits Home page.  Once logged into the system you can upload files to your permit.

  • Submit documents in PDF format only.
  • Submit each document separately. A plan set is one document; a site plan is a separate document.
  • Maximum size of a document is 250MB.
  • A document file name should describe what the document is, for example: plan set, architectural plan set, site plan, structural calculations, etc.
  • Do not use ANY of the following characters in the file name: ! * _ | ; : @ & = = $ , / ? % # [ ] ' " < > |
  • Check the page orientation of ALL the pages in the plan set to ensure that no pages are upside down or flipped on their side.

Electronic Plan Requirements MUST be followed.



Inspections

City offices will be closed for the Labor Day holiday on Monday, September 7. Inspections will not be available that day. Inspections requested by the 2:00 pm deadline on Friday, September 4, will be scheduled on Tuesday, September 8. If your request is received after the 2:00 pm deadline on Friday, or over the holiday weekend, it will be scheduled Wednesday, September 9. Please keep this in mind when making your plans.  

You can view currently scheduled inspections or request an inspection on an existing permit by clicking on one of the buttons below.

To schedule Fire Department inspections, use this link: https://southkingfire.org/326/Permit-Inspections. Please call (253) 946-7348 if you have any questions.



Apply Online

This is where you can apply online for a RESIDENTIAL AND COMMERCIAL permit.  Residential permits that do not require plan review may be applied and paid for with your MasterCard or VISA.  Permits requiring a plan review can be submitted along with supporting plans and documents to start the application and review process.  (See “How to Apply” for more information)

 Effective January 1, 2020 there will be a 3.5% service fee on all credit card transactions.
