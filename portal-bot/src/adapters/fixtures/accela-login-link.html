Warning: Scam Targeting Washington County Permit Applicants

Washington County has had reports of scammers sending fraudulent emails asking customers to wire money for project approval. Washington County will never request wire transfers for permits or payments.

If you receive an unexpected payment request, do not respond. Call 503-846-3470 to verify.

More info here: Protect Yourself from Fake Permit Payment Emails | Washington County, OR
